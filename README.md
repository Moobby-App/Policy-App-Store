# Privacy Policy for Moobby

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026
**Contact Email:** support.moobby@gmail.com
**Privacy Policy URL:** https://moobby-website.vercel.app

Prima Ke 3 ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how Moobby handles information when you use our app.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account & Contact Data:** We do not require account creation or sign-in, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal contact details.
- **Usage & Diagnostics:** We use Firebase Analytics to log fixed event and screen names (e.g., which screens are viewed, feature usage counts). These events never include your precise location, free-text input, or credentials.
- **Device Permissions:** Location — used to show your position relative to nearby commuter line stations and to find the nearest stops across supported transit modes ("When In Use"), and to notify you of disruptions on your route when you arrive at your departure station ("Always"). Push Notifications — used to alert you about disruptions affecting stations you've saved.
- **Device/Installation Data:** To deliver disruption alerts, our server stores an anonymous installation ID, your device's push notification token, the station IDs you've saved, and your chosen alert severity levels. This data is not linked to your name or email, since no account is required.
- **Third-Party SDKs:** Firebase Analytics (Google) for the usage events described above, and Apple Push Notification service (APNs) for delivering alerts.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide and maintain app features (such as disruption alerts and nearby-stop search), fix bugs, and understand feature usage. We do not sell personal data or use it outside the stated purposes.

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app — Firebase/Google (analytics) and Apple (push notification delivery) — or when required by law. All disclosures match our App Store privacy labels.

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep installation and notification data only as long as needed to deliver disruption alerts. You can delete this data at any time by turning off notifications in the Profile tab, which unregisters your device from our server, or by contacting support.moobby@gmail.com. Uninstalling the app removes data stored locally on your device.

## 5. Age Rating & Children's Privacy

Moobby carries an App Store age rating of 16+ (15+ in the Republic of Korea). Moobby is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone below the minimum age shown in their country's App Store listing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically and post the revised version at the Privacy Policy URL above with a new effective date.
